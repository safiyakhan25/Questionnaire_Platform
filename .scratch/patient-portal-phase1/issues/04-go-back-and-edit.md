# 04: Patient can go back and edit an earlier answer

**What to build:** A patient can see a list of sections they've already visited, jump back into any of them, change an answer, and have that change actually saved — without losing their current forward progress.

**Blocked by:** 03 (Patient can answer questions and progress through sections)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The patient can see a list of every section they've already visited, by name
- [ ] Selecting a previously visited section loads that section's questions with their previously given answers pre-filled
- [ ] Changing an answer in a previously visited section and leaving it persists the new answer
- [ ] After editing an earlier section, the patient can return to their current in-progress section without having lost where they were
